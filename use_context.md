# Project Context

## Company Overview

**TalentFlow Solutions** is a mid-sized recruitment technology company founded in 2018, specializing in connecting qualified candidates with employers across various industries. The company processes thousands of job applications monthly and works with over 200 corporate clients ranging from startups to enterprise organizations.

## Business Challenge

TalentFlow's recruitment coordinators currently spend significant time manually reviewing and extracting key information from candidate CVs/resumes. Each recruiter handles approximately 50-100 CVs per day, manually inputting candidate details into their applicant tracking system.

## The Problem

The manual CV processing workflow creates several bottlenecks:

- **Time-intensive**: Recruiters spend 60-70% of their time on data entry rather than candidate engagement and client relationships
- **Inconsistent data quality**: Manual extraction leads to inconsistent formatting and occasional data entry errors
- **Scalability limitations**: The current process cannot efficiently handle peak hiring seasons when application volumes surge
- **Delayed response times**: Candidates often wait 3-5 business days for initial processing before recruiters can properly evaluate their profiles

## Desired Outcome

TalentFlow needs an automated pipeline that can extract structured information from CVs in various formats (PDF, Word documents, etc.) and transform this unstructured data into a consistent, searchable format. This would allow recruiters to focus on high-value activities like candidate screening, interviews, and client communication rather than manual data entry.
